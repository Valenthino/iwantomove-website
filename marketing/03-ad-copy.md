# Ad copy

Use one primary text and one headline per ad. Keep the professional control live while rotating challengers. Availability is always confirmed in conversation; urgency copy does not imply limited slots.

## Relationship humour

- Very short: Keep the couch. Keep the friends. Get a free moving quote.
- Funny: Your friendship has survived a lot. That third-floor couch might be asking too much. Let’s talk about your move.
- Professional: Planning a local home move? Tell IWantToMove.ca where you’re going and request a free quote.
- Stress relief: You can invite your friends over once the couch is in. Start with a free moving quote.

## Friend with a truck

- Very short: Your friend has a truck. We have a quote form. Start there.
- Funny: ‘I know someone with a truck’ is how a lot of very long Saturdays begin. Get a free moving quote.
- Professional: Looking at options for your local move? Share the details and we’ll talk through a quote.
- Convenience: Before you coordinate five friends and a borrowed truck, tell us what needs to move.

## Moving stress

- Very short: Moving list getting longer? Start with a free quote.
- Funny: You’ve packed the kettle. You need the kettle. Moving is a lot. Let’s sort out the moving quote.
- Professional: Request a free quote for local residential or office moving. We’ll call to confirm the details.
- Urgency: Moving date getting closer? Tell us when you’re hoping to move. We’ll discuss your request and availability.

## Couch and furniture

- Very short: Big couch. New address. Get a quote for your home move.
- Funny: It looked smaller in the showroom. Tell us about the couch, the stairs and the rest of your move.
- Professional: Moving home? Include larger furniture and building access in your quote request so we can discuss the details.
- Stress relief: You don’t have to work out every detail before asking. Tell us about your home move and we’ll start there.

## Straightforward professional control

- Very short: Local home and office moving. Get a free quote.
- Professional: Tell IWantToMove.ca where you’re moving, your preferred date and what needs to come with you. We’ll contact you to discuss a quote.
- Trust: A moving quote should start with the details. Let’s go over yours before you decide.
- Convenience: Share your move details in our short form. We’ll call to go over the next steps.

## Stairs and elevators

- Very short: New place. Different stairs. Get a free moving quote.
- Funny: ‘Just a few stairs’ is doing a lot of work in that sentence. Tell us about your building and your move.
- Professional: Planning a local move? Include stairs, elevators and building access in your request so we can go over the details.
- Urgency: Have a moving date in mind? Check your building’s elevator booking rules, then tell us about your move.

## Instant Form copy

Contact permission: By sending this request, you’re asking IWantToMove.ca to contact you about your move.

Review prompt: Please check your phone number and move details before sending.
